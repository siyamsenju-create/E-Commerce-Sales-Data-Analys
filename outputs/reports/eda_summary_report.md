# E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis & Insights
## Executive Analytical Summary Report

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical distribution evaluation, and enterprise data quality audit of the **Superstore E-Commerce Dataset** ($9,994$ transaction line items across $5,009$ customer orders spanning $2014$ through $2017$).

### Key Analytical Highlights:
- **Financial Baseline:** Generated **$2,297,200.86** in gross sales and **$286,397.02** in total net profit, representing an overall commercial profit margin of **12.47%**.
- **Transaction Granularity:** The dataset comprises **5,009 unique checkout orders** generated by **793 unique customer accounts**. The Average Order Value (**AOV**) is **$458.61** (median order value: **$151.96**), while the average individual line-item sales value is **$229.86** (median: **$54.49**).
- **Distribution Characteristics:** Raw sales and profit exhibit strong positive (right) skewness ($S_{\text{sales}} = +12.97$, $S_{\text{profit}} = +7.56$). A logarithmic transformation produces a substantially more symmetric representation.
- **Category Profitability Dynamics:** Technology generated the highest total revenue ($836,154.03, 36.40% share) and delivered **50.79% of all net profit ($145,454.95)** with a **17.40% margin**. In contrast, Furniture generated $741,999.80 (32.30% of sales) but yielded only **6.44% of net profit ($18,451.27)** with a **2.49% margin**, coinciding with heavy losses in Tables (-$17,725.48 net loss).
- **Discount Association:** Applied discounts and net profitability show a moderate negative linear association ($r = -0.2195$). Transactions with discounts $\le 20\%$ yielded **+$421,773.08** in total profit, whereas transactions with discounts $\ge 30\%$ accumulated **-$135,376.06** in cumulative net losses.
- **Linear Associations:** Quantity and sales exhibit a weak positive linear association ($r = +0.2008$). Unit price variation across product lines accounts for the dominant portion of line-item revenue variance.
- **Time-Series Trajectory:** Annual revenue expanded consistently across 2014–2017. Sales volume accelerated in September and reached recurring annual peaks during the fourth quarter (October, November, and December).

## 3. Data Quality Assessment & Methodology

A non-destructive data quality audit was conducted prior to analytical modeling:

- **Missing Values:** 0 null values across all 21 raw features (100% complete).
- **Duplicate Records:** 0 exact duplicate rows across all fields.
- **Quantity Boundary Validity:** All records satisfy $\text{Quantity} \ge 1$ (Range: $1$ to $14$ units).
- **Sales Boundary Validity:** All records satisfy $\text{Sales} \ge \$0.44$ (no negative gross revenue).
- **Discount Range Validity:** All records conform to the expected range $[0.00, 0.80]$ ($0\%$ to $80\%$).
- **Chronological Logic:** Across all 9,994 records, `ship_date >= order_date` holds with 0 date sequence errors.
- **Outlier Assessment (IQR Method):**
  - `sales`: 1,167 potential upper outliers (11.68%), upper threshold: **$498.93** (max: $22,638.48).
  - `profit`: 1,881 potential outliers (18.82%), bounds: **[-$39.72, +$70.82]** (min: -$6,599.98, max: +$8,399.98).
- **Data Retention Rationale:** No records were deleted. High-ticket purchases represent genuine commercial enterprise orders, and negative profits represent legitimate business loss events resulting from steep promotional discounting.

## 14. Evidence-Based Insights

1. **Technology Drives Majority Profitability:** Technology generated 50.79% of total net profits ($145,454.95) on 36.40% of sales.
2. **Furniture Suffers Margin Dilution:** Furniture delivered only 6.44% of total profit ($18,451.27) on 32.30% of sales, with Tables operating at an aggregate net loss of -$17,725.48.
3. **Discounts $\ge 30\%$ Associated with Cumulative Losses:** High discount tiers systematically coincide with negative aggregate margins across sub-categories.
4. **Late-Year Demand Concentration:** Volume accelerates in September and peaks during Q4 (November/December).
5. **Top-Line Volume Does Not Equal Net Margin:** High-revenue entities (such as Sean Miller and the Cisco TelePresence system) produced net negative returns in this dataset.

## 15. Limitations & Confounding Factors

- **Observational Data:** Findings describe associations and do not prove causality.
- **Unmeasured Logistics & Storage Costs:** Shipping carrier costs and warehousing fees are not explicitly tracked in the dataset.
- **Linear Correlation Scope:** Pearson $r$ evaluates linear relationships only.

### Data Leakage Safeguards:
- Models predicting order outcomes must only use features available at checkout request time (e.g., historical customer RFM, product catalog metadata) rather than post-order transaction variables.
- Scaling and transformations must be fit strictly on training splits.
